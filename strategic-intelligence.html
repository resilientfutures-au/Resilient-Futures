Why We Publish

Reading the conditions sits at the heart of how we work.

The intent

Help leaders read change earlier, name what they’re seeing more precisely, and decide better.

Strategic intelligence — the disciplined, evidence-based reading of the conditions an organisation is operating in — sits at the heart of how we work.

It’s the first concept in Strategy in Action (SiA): every engagement starts with building a shared, current view of what’s actually happening before any strategic decisions are made.

Publishing extends that practice. It makes the thinking, the frameworks, and the patterns we observe available to a much wider community of leaders — many of whom we will never work with directly, but who are grappling with the same structural conditions our clients face.

Our Thinking

What’s different about what you’ll find here.

Three things to know about how this body of work is made — and what it isn’t.

A · Source of Material

Practitioner depth, not commentary.

What we publish comes out of actual work with Boards and Executive teams — not desk research, not paraphrased trend reports, not material produced for the act of publishing itself.

B · The Time Frame

Tested across cycles, not just this one.

25 years of practice in complex adaptive systems, resilience science, and cognitive science means our framing has been tested against multiple cycles of disruption.

The current moment is not our first encounter with structural change.

C · The Bar

Built to be useful.

The intent is to help leaders see and act — not to demonstrate thought leadership credentials.

If a piece isn’t sharpening someone’s strategic clarity, it doesn’t belong here.
