About the Book

A guidebook to the exponential era.

The Question

What are our disruptors, and how will we leverage them?

Disrupted is a distillation of Resilient Futures’ Strategy in Action (SiA), a framework designed to help teams and leaders leverage, rather than fall prey to, exponential disruptive change.

It’s a guidebook to the exponential era and a toolbox for proactively leveraging it. Through well-known disruption-opportunists like Apple and Tesla, Disrupted shows readers the kind of strategic thinking that lets teams and leaders confidently pursue opportunities, with a shared strategic language and a methodology designed for timely, agile execution at every level of their organisation.

Written in 2015, before the convergence of disruption forces became the dominant operating condition, and still a useful frame for what’s now unmistakably here.
