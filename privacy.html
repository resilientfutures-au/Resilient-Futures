Your Privacy

Privacy Policy

Resilient Futures is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 9 July 2026

Resilient Futures (“Resilient Futures”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This policy describes how we collect, use, store, and disclose personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or engage our services.

Information we collect

We only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary for our work with you. This may include:

  • Information you give us directly — your name, email address, phone number, organisation, role, and the content of any message you send when you complete a form, book a conversation, or email us.
  • Engagement information — details you share with us during workshops, advisory sessions, and programmes so we can deliver our services.
  • Technical information — when you use our website, our providers may automatically collect limited technical data such as your browser type, device, and pages viewed, to keep the site secure and understand how it is used.

How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • respond to your enquiries and schedule conversations;
  • deliver, manage, and improve our services and programmes;
  • send you information you have requested or that is relevant to an engagement;
  • meet our legal, accounting, and record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to those set out above without your consent.

Forms, scheduling, and third-party services

Our contact form and meeting-booking links are provided by HubSpot, which acts as our customer relationship management (CRM) and forms provider. When you submit a form or book a call, the information you provide is processed and stored by HubSpot on our behalf. We also use HubSpot’s analytics to understand how our website is used and to recognise returning visitors; for this purpose HubSpot sets cookies that may collect information such as pages viewed and how you arrived at the site. You can review HubSpot’s handling of data in the HubSpot Privacy Policy.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use cookies that are necessary for the website and its embedded forms to function correctly and securely, and analytics cookies (set by HubSpot) that help us measure how the site is used. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when they are used; some parts of the site, such as the contact form, may not work fully if cookies are disabled.

Disclosure of your information

We may disclose your personal information to:

  • service providers who help us operate our business and website (such as HubSpot), under obligations of confidentiality;
  • professional advisers where reasonably required; and
  • government or regulatory bodies where we are required or authorised by law.

Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers, including HubSpot, store data on servers located outside Australia (including in the United States). Where we disclose personal information overseas, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. When personal information is no longer needed for any purpose for which it may be used or disclosed, we take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify it.

Accessing and correcting your information

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date, or incomplete. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Complaints

If you believe we have breached the Australian Privacy Principles or mishandled your personal information, please contact us first so we can address your concern. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page, with the “last updated” date shown above.

Contact us

For any questions about this policy or how we handle your personal information, email us at info@resilientfutures.com.
