Applying Strategy in Action to your organisation.

Strategy in Action (SiA) is Resilient Futures' unique strategy framework — designed for formulating agile strategy and timely responses in our current disruptive environment.

Seven service packages, each a different way to engage with and embed SiA. Every starting point is tailored to your organisation's needs.

The Approach

Every organisation has a unique starting point.

That starting point acts as a 'proof-of-value' project — one that provides you with immediate, implementable outcomes and demonstrates how we work. All of our services are delivered in person and through online facilitation, and come with instructional materials.

Based on the outcomes of the proof-of-value project, we begin to design a pathway of growth and a program of specific services that will take you toward being able to future-proof your organisation. We do this by packaging services specifically based on your organisation's needs.

Every program is custom made.

Not sure where to start? A SiA Kick-Start or Hack is typically the right first conversation. It gives your leadership team direct exposure to the framework and creates the clarity needed to design a longer program.

Ideal for organisations that are

In a current, or perceiving a future state of turbulent change as a result of known or emerging disruptive conditions

Undergoing transformational change

Wanting agile strategy and projects that deliver real outcomes

Seeking to innovate without a clear strategic framing for that work

Valuing customer-centric transformation

Hit by a major change and needing immediate action

Service Packages

Seven ways to engage with Strategy in Action.

From a three-hour orientation to a full organisational embedding program. Each service is a distinct engagement — and each can serve as the proof-of-value starting point for a broader program.

01

SiA Kick-Start

3-hour session

An excellent place to start strategy work. You'll get an understanding of disruption and be introduced to Strategy in Action as a framework that enables organisations to embrace and take advantage of disruption. Participants typically wake up to the reality that different thinking and action really is required.

Well suited to ensuring your executive team, leadership group, or team is aligned on the need for a new approach to strategy formulation and implementation.

02

SiA Hack

1 day

A dynamic one-day application of Strategy in Action for organisations that need to crack open a problem and rapidly find solutions or progress opportunities. A hands-on execution that exposes participants to the framework while quickly hacking through to key actions and delivering definite viable pathways forward.

Works well for teams seeking to expose, evaluate, and prioritise a range of options, or for those seeking to unclog a blockage in an existing project or program.

03

SiA Project

3 × ½-day

Teaches you the basics of Strategy in Action by focussing on a project that is in formulation, being planned, or already underway. This project-based approach ensures you'll learn and apply the framework to an area of immediate focus — enabling you to learn the concepts while enhancing the delivery of work already underway.

Can also be used to sense-check the focus and validity of projects that are not gaining traction or delivering expected results.

04

SiA Accelerator

6 × ½-day

Provides in-depth working knowledge of Strategy in Action through a learning-in-action approach. Participants apply SiA across six workshops to analyse immediate and emergent conditions, assess strategic opportunity-risks, and validate opportunities as part of making a case about where to focus.

Ideal for organisations seeking to build, empower, and connect genuine strategic capability at all levels — helping embed strategic agility as a skill that can be passed on throughout the broader organisation.

05

SiA Facilitation

Custom program

A facilitated formulation of strategy through the application of SiA, supporting the activation of a refreshed strategic focus. You are guided through the analysis of conditions and the identification of strategic opportunity-risks (SORs) — then validated against an existing strategy or used to chart a new strategic direction.

Resilient Futures also acts as an objective observer throughout, providing feedback and critique on the quality of thinking and outputs driving the strategic focus.

06

Capability Analysis and Mapping

Custom program

Determines which future-focused capabilities need to be developed or acquired, and which current capabilities need to be retired. These decisions are best made when an organisation clearly understands both its current capabilities and future requirements. Resilient Futures coaches and supports you to develop internal competence to map and analyse capability.

Perfect for organisations looking to speed up processes, break through silos, identify and free up blockages, and unlock new value.

07

SiA Embedding

A customised approach to developing and embedding the capability needed for SiA to be implemented within an organisation at all key levels. While a specific approach and program can be designed to support your organisation's particular requirements, it is often best to start with a SiA Hack accelerator to develop an insight into how SiA is applied.

SiA Embedding works best for organisations seeking to reframe and refocus their core approach to business operations — building strategic capability that endures at every level.

The full embedding includes
Process development
Team establishment
Team leadership development
Team training
Building competency, capacity, and commitment

Enables your organisation to authentically empower people at all levels to engage with the strategic focus of the business.
